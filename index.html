BIS Certification 2026: Types, Process, Requirements, Fees & Validity

BIS Certification is a conformity assessment system that helps ensure products meet the applicable Indian Standards. The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) is India's national standards body and operates under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution. Depending on the product and applicable regulatory requirements, manufacturers may need BIS certification before selling or importing certain products in India.

BIS certification helps manufacturers demonstrate product conformity, improve consumer confidence and meet applicable regulatory requirements. The certification route depends on the product category, relevant Indian Standard and applicable Quality Control Order or other regulatory provisions.

What Is the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)?

The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) is the national standards body of India, established under the BIS Act, 1986 and continued under the BIS Act, 2016. It develops and publishes Indian Standards, operates product certification schemes, conducts conformity assessment activities and supports quality assurance across various industries.

BIS certification is granted by BIS after the applicable assessment requirements have been fulfilled. These may include product testing, factory inspection, documentation review and other scheme-specific requirements.

Types of BIS Certification Schemes

BIS operates different conformity assessment schemes for products and manufacturers. The applicable scheme depends on the product, Indian Standard and relevant regulatory notification.

BIS Certification Process: Step-by-Step

Identify the Applicable Indian Standard

The first step is to identify the correct Indian Standard for the product. The manufacturer should also verify whether the product is covered by a mandatory QCO or another applicable regulatory requirement.

Determine the Applicable Certification Scheme

The certification route is selected according to the product category, applicable Indian Standard and BIS conformity assessment requirements. This may include Scheme I, Scheme II, Scheme IV or Scheme X, where applicable.

Prepare the Required Documents

The manufacturer prepares the application and supporting documents, which may include company registration details, manufacturing address, product specifications, test reports, machinery details and quality control information, depending on the scheme.

Submit the Application

The application is submitted through the applicable BIS application process or portal, along with the required information and prescribed fees.

Product Testing

Samples are tested in a BIS-recognised laboratory or through the testing route prescribed for the relevant scheme. The product must meet the applicable Indian Standard requirements.

Factory Inspection

Where required, BIS officials assess the manufacturing facility, production process, testing arrangements and quality control system. The exact assessment requirements depend on the certification scheme.

Review and Grant of Certification

BIS reviews the application, test results, inspection findings and other applicable requirements. If the requirements are fulfilled, BIS may grant the relevant licence, registration or Certificate of Conformity.

Documents Required for BIS Certification

  • Company registration certificate
  • Manufacturing unit address proof
  • Product specifications and technical details
  • Manufacturing process flow chart
  • List of manufacturing machinery
  • List of testing equipment and calibration records
  • Quality control and inspection details
  • Raw material specifications
  • Relevant product test reports, where applicable
  • Authorisation documents and declarations, as required

Timeline for BIS Certification

The time required for BIS certification varies according to the product, certification scheme, laboratory testing requirements, completeness of documentation and factory inspection process. Any timeline provided before reviewing the product and scheme should be treated as an estimate rather than a guaranteed approval period.

Validity and Renewal of BIS Certification

The validity and renewal conditions depend on the applicable BIS certification scheme and the terms of the licence, registration or Certificate of Conformity. Manufacturers should check the validity period, renewal requirements, surveillance conditions and applicable fees for their specific certification.

Role of a BIS Consultant

A BIS consultant can assist manufacturers with identifying the applicable Indian Standard, selecting the relevant certification route, preparing documents, coordinating product testing, preparing for factory inspections and responding to application-related queries. A consultant provides professional support but does not issue BIS licences or certificates. Certification decisions and approvals are made by BIS.

Frequently Asked Questions About BIS Certification

Who issues BIS Certification in India?

BIS certification is issued by the Bureau of Indian Standards, India's national standards body, subject to the applicable conformity assessment requirements.

Is BIS Certification mandatory for all products?

No. BIS certification is mandatory only for products covered by applicable compulsory certification requirements, such as relevant Quality Control Orders. Other products may follow voluntary certification routes.

Can foreign manufacturers apply for BIS Certification?

Yes. Eligible foreign manufacturers can apply under the applicable BIS certification route, including the Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme for products covered by its scope.

How much does BIS Certification cost?

The cost varies by product, scheme, laboratory testing, inspection and applicable BIS fees. A product-specific estimate is needed to calculate the total cost.

How long does BIS Certification take?

The timeline depends on the product, testing requirements, documentation, inspection and BIS review. There is no single fixed timeline for every certification.

Can an importer obtain BIS Certification?

For product certification routes that require manufacturer licensing or registration, the application is generally made by the manufacturer. Importers should verify the specific product requirements and applicable scheme.

What is the difference between BIS and ISI Mark?

BIS is the national standards body and certification authority. The ISI Mark is a conformity mark used on products covered by the relevant BIS licence under the applicable certification scheme.
